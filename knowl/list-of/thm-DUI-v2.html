Theorem 6.1.3.

Let \(f(x, y)\) be a function on a rectangle \(R=\{(x, y): a\le x \le b, \; c\le y\le d\}\) such that both \(f\) and \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\) are continuous on \(R.\) Let \(a\) and \(b\) be differentiable functions on \([c, d]\) such that \(a\le a(y), b(y) \le b\) for all \(y\) in \([c, d].\) Then
\begin{equation*} \frac{d}{dy}\int_{a(y)}^{b(y)} f(x, y)\, dx = b'(y)\, f(b(y), y) - a'(y) \,f(a(y), y)+\int_{a(y)}^{b(y)} \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x, y) \, dx. \end{equation*}
